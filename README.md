F# ⚽ Football Owner Simulator

A browser-based football club management simulator where you become the owner of your own football club.

Build your club from the lower divisions of English football and work your way toward the top.

## 🎮 About the Game

Football Owner Simulator puts you in the role of a club owner rather than just a manager.

You are responsible for:

- Club finances
- Transfers
- Player contracts
- Managers
- Scouting
- Youth academy
- Sponsors
- Club facilities
- League progression
- Cup competitions
- Board confidence
- Fan support
- Club reputation

Every decision can have consequences.

## ✨ Features

### 🏟️ Club Management
- Create your own owner
- Create your own football club
- Choose your starting division
- Custom club colours
- Club value system
- Fan support
- Board confidence
- Owner reputation
- Media reputation

### 👥 Squad Management
- Player transfers
- Player sales
- Contract renewals
- Player wages
- Market values
- Player morale
- Player form
- Injuries
- Division-based OVR eligibility

### 🔎 Scouting
Player ratings are hidden before scouting.

Scout players to discover:

- Overall Rating
- Potential
- Estimated Market Value
- Scout Recommendation

Better scouts provide more accurate reports.

### 🌱 Youth Academy
Develop young prospects through the academy.

Academy players can:

- Develop over time
- Be scouted
- Reveal their potential
- Be promoted to the first team

### 🧠 Managers
Hire and replace managers with different strengths.

Manager attributes include:

- Tactics
- Youth Development
- Motivation
- Experience

### 💰 Sponsorship
Negotiate commercial contracts with different sponsors.

Sponsors can affect:

- Club income
- Fan support
- Media reputation
- Board confidence

Some sponsors may provide greater financial rewards while causing negative public reactions.

### 🏆 Competitions
Climb through the English football pyramid.

Current divisions:

1. Premier League
2. Championship
3. League One
4. League Two
5. National League

The game also includes:

- FA Cup
- Carabao Cup
- Promotion
- Relegation
- League tables
- Matchweeks
- Points system

### 🎲 Match Simulation

Matches are simulated using a weighted RNG system.

Team strength is influenced by:

- Player Overall
- Player Form
- Player Morale
- Manager Tactics
- Home Advantage
- Opponent Strength

Higher-rated teams generally have a higher probability of winning, but unexpected results can still happen.

### 💾 Career Saves

Career files can be:

- Saved
- Loaded
- Deleted
- Automatically saved

Save data currently uses browser storage.

## 🖥️ Tech Stack

This project currently uses:

- HTML5
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript
- LocalStorage

No framework or backend is currently required.
